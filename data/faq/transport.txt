Getting to the Airport
Passengers can reach the airport by taxi, airport shuttle, private car or public bus. Travel time from Colombo is typically 45 to 90 minutes depending on traffic, so allow extra time during peak hours.

Taxis
Licensed airport taxis are available at the arrivals exit. Fixed-fare counters are located inside the arrivals hall. Use only licensed operators.

Pick-up and Drop-off
Private vehicles can use the drop-off zone at departures for short stops only. Longer waiting should be done in the car park.
