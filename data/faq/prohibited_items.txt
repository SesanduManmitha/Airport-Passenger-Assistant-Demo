SAMPLE CONTENT (mock data for practice, not official policy)

Prohibited Items in Cabin Baggage
Sharp objects such as knives and scissors, firearms and replicas, explosives, flammable liquids and aerosols above the allowed size are not permitted in cabin baggage.

Liquids
Liquids, gels and creams in cabin baggage must be in containers of 100 ml or less, placed together in one transparent resealable bag of about 1 litre. Baby milk and essential medicines are allowed in reasonable quantities.

Batteries
Power banks and spare lithium batteries must be carried in cabin baggage, not in checked baggage.

Duty Free
Duty free liquids bought at the airport are allowed if sealed in a tamper-evident bag with the receipt.
