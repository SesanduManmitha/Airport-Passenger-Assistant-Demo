SAMPLE CONTENT (mock data for practice, not official policy)

Baggage Allowance
Economy passengers can usually check in one bag up to 23 kg. Business class passengers can usually check in two bags up to 32 kg each. Allowances vary by airline and fare type, so passengers should confirm with their airline.

Cabin Baggage
One cabin bag up to 7 kg is generally allowed, with a maximum size of about 55 x 35 x 25 cm. A small personal item such as a handbag or laptop bag is also usually allowed.

Excess and Oversized Baggage
Bags over the allowance are charged at the airline's excess baggage rate, payable at the check-in counter. Sports equipment and musical instruments should be arranged with the airline in advance.

Lost or Delayed Baggage
Report missing bags to the airline's baggage services desk in the arrivals hall before leaving the terminal. Keep your baggage tag and boarding pass.
