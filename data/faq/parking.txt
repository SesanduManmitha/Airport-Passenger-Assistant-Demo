SAMPLE CONTENT (mock data for practice, not official policy)

Car Parking
Short-term and long-term parking are available near the terminal. Short-term parking is charged per hour, and long-term parking is charged per day.

Payment
Pay at the parking kiosk or the exit booth before leaving. Keep your parking ticket safe, since a lost ticket may incur a fixed charge.

Accessible Parking
Reserved spaces for passengers with reduced mobility are located closest to the terminal entrance.
