SAMPLE CONTENT (mock data for practice, not official policy)

Check-in Times
Check-in typically opens 3 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure for international flights. Arrive at least 3 hours early.

Documents Required
A valid passport, visa if required, and a ticket or e-ticket confirmation are needed at check-in. Passports should be valid for at least 6 months.

Online Check-in
Many airlines offer online check-in 24 to 48 hours before departure. Passengers with only cabin baggage can go directly to security and immigration.

Transit Passengers
Passengers connecting to another flight should follow the transit signs after arrival and check their onward gate on the information screens.
